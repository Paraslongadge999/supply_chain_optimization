# Supply Chain Optimization  
A data-driven exploration of supply chain excellence

---

## 1. Why This Matters  
Modern supply chains face unpredictable demand, operational bottlenecks, and rising costs. This project is built to tackle those challenges using analytics, forecasting, and automation.  
Instead of just showing dashboards, the goal here is simple: **turn data into operational decisions** that reduce delays, improve inventory efficiency, and strengthen the entire flow of goods.

---

## 2. My Journey & Purpose  
This project started with a clear intention: build something that actually helps decision-makers—not just looks good in a notebook.  
I created this repository to:  
- Transform raw operational data into meaningful insights.  
- Build forecasting and optimization workflows that reduce uncertainty.  
- Demonstrate real business impact: fewer stockouts, lower costs, faster operations.  

This is more than another portfolio project. It’s a **practical demonstration** of using data science to improve supply chain performance end-to-end.

---

## 3. What’s Inside  
- **Demand Forecasting** – Predict future product needs to avoid stockouts or overstocking.  
- **Inventory Planning** – Optimize reorder points, safety stock, and inventory rotation.  
- **Bottleneck Detection** – Identify slow stages in the supply chain and propose improvements.  
- **Cost Reduction Levers** – Analyze transportation, lead times, and supplier performance to cut waste.  
- **Analytics & Automation Stack** – Includes notebooks, scripts, and processing logic.  
- **requirements.txt** – All dependencies listed for quick setup.
